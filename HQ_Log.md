## Vision and mission
**Vision:** a South Africa where every small business, not just big companies, has access to smart technology that helps it thrive.

**Mission:** build smart software and AI systems that save small businesses time, win them more customers and take the stress out of running them, with honest pricing and local support.

**Values:** results over hype, honesty, responsibility for data (POPIA), and partnership after launch.

**Tagline:** Your gateway to smarter business.
