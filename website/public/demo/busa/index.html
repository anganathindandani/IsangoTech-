Welcome to Beacon Bay

Warm, personal hospitality across 43 rooms and suites

The Busa Guest House Group brings together four guesthouses in Beacon Bay, East London. Whether you need a 4-star bed and breakfast with river views, an affordable self-catering unit, or a whole house for the family, there’s a Busa stay that fits.

You’re minutes from Nahoon Beach, Beacon Bay Crossing and the East London CBD, with King Phalo Airport about 20 minutes away.
