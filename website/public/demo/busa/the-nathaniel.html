About this stay

Space for the whole family

The Nathaniel is made for families, groups and anyone who wants room to breathe. The Main House sleeps eight, with an open-plan kitchen, a sunny lounge looking onto the garden, and a covered patio.

Alongside it, the private Deluxe Queen units are perfect for couples or business travellers who want their own front door and a kitchenette.
