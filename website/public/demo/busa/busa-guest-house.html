About this stay

Our flagship, high above the Nahoon River

Busa Guest House sits in a quiet street in Beacon Bay North, with views across the Nahoon River valley to the sea. It’s the home of the group and the place most of our regulars come back to.

Breakfast is served in the dining room each morning, the lapa and braai are ready for evenings outdoors, and the garden and pool are yours to enjoy. With 13 rooms, from twins to a four-poster king and self-catering units, it works for business trips, couples and families alike.
