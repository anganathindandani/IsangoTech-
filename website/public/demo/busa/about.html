Our story

From one guesthouse to four

Busa began with a single guesthouse in Beacon Bay North and a simple idea: treat every guest like family.

Over the years the group has grown to include 65 Pell and The Nathaniel, with The McVicar on the way. Each guesthouse has its own character, but the welcome is the same: personal, warm and proudly Eastern Cape.
