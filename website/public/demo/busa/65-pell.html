About this stay

Modern, private and great value

65 Pell is a quiet, modern self-catering guesthouse in Blue Bend. Every unit opens onto a covered patio around the pool courtyard and has its own kitchenette, so you can come and go as you please.

It’s the group’s most affordable option, and a favourite with contractors on longer work trips, couples and anyone who likes their independence.
